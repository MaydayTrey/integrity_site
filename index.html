Meet Phil

Raised on hard work

Farm kid from Preble County, family man in Trenton, and the one holding the trowel on every job.

  1. 01 · Roots

    A farm kid from Preble County

    Phil grew up on a farm in Preble County, where the day started before the sun and nothing got done unless you did it yourself. Fences, equipment, barns, whatever broke got fixed, and fixed right, because you'd be the one using it tomorrow. That is where he learned to work, and it never left him.

    He carried that into the family he and his wife are raising in Trenton. Four kids, a full house, and the same rule he grew up with: show up, do the work, take care of your people.

  2. 02 · Craft

    The same standard, in your home

    That work ethic is what you see in the tile. Phil is on every job himself, from demo to the last bead of caulk, and he does not call something finished until it is the way he would want it in his own house. Straight lines, tight grout, no shortcuts where nobody will look.

    He also knows what it means to let a stranger into your home, because he is a husband and a dad first. Your family's routine keeps going, the job site stays clean, and you always know what happens next. That is the integrity in the name.
